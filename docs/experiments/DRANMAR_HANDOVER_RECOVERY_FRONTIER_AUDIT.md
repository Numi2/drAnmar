## What the audit ruled out

- v10 discrete recovery switching destroyed incumbent trajectories.
- v11 receiver residual improved only 4/2,000, below the promotion floor.
- v12 controlled the wrong actuator before presentation.
- v13 learned giver correction fought the competent nominal servo.
- global ramp and raised receiver preposition delayed acquisition.
- force projection used a noisy instantaneous signal.
- unconditional receiver deceleration removed useful acquisitions.
- a reactive soft-force shield acted too late.
- a 12 mm tool-tip barrier did not intersect the collision path.

## Proven remaining gap

Pre-reset attribution proves the receiver crosses 2 N in all five frontier
safety terminals. Four occur after lift and before acquisition. Current
contact sensors distinguish needle force from all other force, but do not
identify the other collider.

The next experiment must add collider-level filters for:

1. receiver jaw versus giver tool;
2. receiver jaw versus support surface; and
3. receiver jaw versus remaining scene geometry.

Only after that attribution should control change again. This preserves the
1,164/2,000 frontier and prevents another blind parameter sweep.

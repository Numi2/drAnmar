# Contact-Qualified Recovery Transport v15

## Hypothesis

The 0.08 recovery carry limit produced the strongest downstream result:
1,164/2,000 retained handovers, 1,485 stable presentations, and 1,236 receiver
contacts. Its six protected-surface terminals failed safety non-inferiority.
The safer 0.07 limit passed the paired 2,000-environment gate but did not
reproduce on the 600-environment development population.

The missing structure is that one limit was serving two physically different
states:

- relift or uncertain custody close to the support surface; and
- already-lifted, live bilateral custody transporting toward presentation.

## Controller

v15 preserves the qualified 0.06 lateral limit during relift and whenever live
bilateral giver contact is absent. It enables 0.08 only when all three
conditions hold:

1. the episode is in pickup-recovery context;
2. the physical progress phase is at least lifted custody; and
3. both giver jaws currently contact the needle.

This is a state-dependent action bound, not a reward or termination change.
First-attempt trajectories, receiver control, jaw closure, giver release,
success, deadline, and safety terminals are unchanged.

## Gate

v15 must exceed both 390/600 and 1,130/2,000 on the exact incumbent
populations. Drops and excessive object-force terminals must remain zero, and
the protected-surface rate may increase by at most 0.1 percentage points.
Only then can it enter the preregistered multi-seed promotion gate.

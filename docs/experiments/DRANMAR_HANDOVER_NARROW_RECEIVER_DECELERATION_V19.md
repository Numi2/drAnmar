# Narrow Recovered Receiver Deceleration v19

v18 proved final-approach deceleration can eliminate the observed receiver
collision signal, but a 6 mm zone at 0.05 authority erased the development
success gain.

v19 changes only that safety projection:

- activation begins inside 4 mm rather than 6 mm;
- translation authority is 0.075 rather than 0.05; and
- only recovered receiver approach is affected.

The outer approach, 25 mm preposition, curved-needle grasp frame, orientation,
giver transport, grippers, release, reward, success, deadline, and safety
terminations remain unchanged. The same 393/600 development and 1,140/2,000
scale floors apply, with no more than four protected-surface terminals.

# Mechanism

The end effector replaces the Panda hand and registers optical, Doppler, and ultrasound sensing around one assessment TCP. Twelve driven joints position the spectral filter wheel, optical focus, speckle mirrors, ultrasound probe, Doppler probe, gel valve, and compliant guard.

The instrument is category-level and manufacturer-neutral. Dimensions, drive gains, contact settings, and masses are provisional research values.

# DrAnmar asset realism audit and B/A promotion blueprint

Date: 27 July 2026  
Method: source-only code, contract, asset, and evidence review  
Execution performed: none

## Bottom line

No DrAnmar asset currently earns an overall A for realism. Several assets now
have a defensible B-range source architecture because their outcome paths are
causal, fail closed, and require exact scene evidence. That is not the same as
B evidence. For every source changed in this review, old runtime artifacts are
historical and do not qualify the modified revision.

The highest-confidence physical implementation is the Warp braided suture at
B- source / C+ evidence. The strongest authority-separation architecture is the
Cosmos-H-Dreams support path at B+ source / C static evidence, but that component
is perception/prediction support and can never be physical ground truth.

Many workcell architectures now reject caller-authored success, force,
injury, retention, patency, viability, seal, cut, and recovery claims. Their
remaining common blocker is the missing native bridge that must populate the
new evidence envelopes from real post-physics state. SafePlane is fail-closed
but does not yet have such an adapter. The Dynamic Patient and Autonomous
Rescue OR remain C-range because their public authority/transaction boundaries
are incomplete; honest abstention does not raise deployable realism.

This audit does not claim clinical validation, physics calibration, regulatory
approval, or suitability for patient-care use.

## Grade definitions

Two independent grades are required.

### Source realism

- **A**: physically complete at its declared scope; native state is
  authoritative; conservation, coupling, failure, uncertainty, and calibrated
  parameters are implemented; no outcome-relevant proxy bypass exists.
- **B**: causal state-derived mechanics with explicit limits; exact identity,
  clock, topology, and provenance; relevant conservation and failure modes;
  outcome paths fail closed when evidence is absent.
- **C**: coherent engineering proxy or partial physical route, but one or more
  decisive mechanisms are prescribed, kinematic, heuristic, or uncoupled.
- **D**: task state or outcomes can be authored, inferred from synthetic labels,
  or produced without the claimed physical mechanism.
- **F**: the source contradicts its claim or reports an effect that the
  mechanics cannot produce.

### Evidence realism

- **A**: repeated, revision-bound native runs plus held-out instrumented
  physical correlation; calibrated uncertainty; representative operating and
  failure regimes.
- **B**: repeated, revision-bound native task telemetry with raw records,
  convergence evidence, failures, and complete runtime/hardware/asset identity.
- **C**: useful engineering evidence, but incomplete revision identity,
  repetition, raw traces, convergence, or physical correlation.
- **D**: static/source evidence, fixture-only results, stale results, or no
  native evidence for the current source.
- **F**: evidence claims an effect that was not observed.

Claim integrity is not a substitute for either grade. A component may be
excellent at refusing false claims while still having C mechanics and D
evidence.

### Coverage accounting

The grading unit is each independently claimed mechanics, sensing, patient,
task, or outcome authority. Payload/standalone/rigid-proxy USD variants,
cartridges, pads, fluids, individual patient-organ meshes, GLB previews, and
camera scenes do not create separate outcome authorities; they inherit the
grade and evidence boundary of their owning row. PSM, ECM, STAR, and the closure
coordinator are listed separately because their dynamics or orchestration
constrain multiple assets. This prevents a catalog file count from disguising
one shared missing mechanism as dozens of separately “passing” assets.

## Cross-portfolio corrections implemented in this source review

### One evidence language

`source/extensions/orbit.surgical.assets/orbit/surgical/assets/scene_evidence.py`
now provides immutable measurements with exact source prim, target prim,
contact pair, attachment IDs, raw record IDs, episode, environment, topology
revision, physics step, simulation time, interval, adapter identity, and a
canonical digest.

Asset-specific envelope/admission code now exists for wound preparation,
exposure, adaptive hemostasis, anastomosis, seal/divide, patient contact,
resuscitation, rescue, and perfusion. At their strongest boundaries they reject
missing or extra sources, wrong prims/contact pairs/attachments, replay, mixed
episodes/environments/topology, and clock discontinuity. These Python objects
are still caller-constructible: internal consistency is not proof that a native
post-physics provider issued the record.

### Shared causal mechanics

`source/extensions/orbit.surgical.assets/orbit/surgical/physics/` now contains
shared reduced-order contact, rod, soft-tissue, vessel, clip, and cohesive
mechanics. Important improvements include:

- orientation-consistent contact identity and tangential vectors;
- dissipative Coulomb friction rather than caller-authored friction outcomes;
- rod bending energy, reactions, damage, fracture, and failed-edge removal;
- tissue reactions, localized wound/tract state, damage, and failed-element
  removal;
- vessel flow, leakage, wall state, stored volume, and continuity residual;
- clip forming/contact/retention capacity separated from measured tangential
  load and slip;
- cohesive traction, energy, damage, and failure tied to an exact interface.

These are reduced models, not full surgical physics. The rod is not yet a fully
objective director-based Cosserat formulation; the vessel is quasi-steady 1D;
the tissue model has no explicit crack faces/node separation; the clip is a
scalar-gap forming model; and cohesive interfaces do not by themselves create
native topology.

### Shared patient authority, still incomplete

Dynamic patient, rescue, resuscitation, and workcell state now route through
one `PatientAuthority` for blood/fluid effects. The former authored intervention
registry/robot adapter is quarantined and cannot be instantiated; caller-authored
incision and wound-grasp qualification now fail closed. Contact stepping
requires a complete next-interval batch with shared episode, environment,
topology, step, and time. This is not yet a B authority: the patient exposes
mutable low-level models and authority methods, commits are not atomic, bile and
generic suction do not debit source compartments, and native provider identity
is absent.

### False success paths closed

- Stapler deployment no longer implies retained closure.
- Closure motion no longer sets cure or closure success.
- Oncology public fusion, removal, ablation, sealing, release, injury,
  registration, cavity, blood-loss, phase-completion, finalization,
  observation, and reward shortcuts fail closed.
- Perfusion viability and patency claims remain false; synthetic generators are
  fixture-named and cannot enter the production verifier.
- Exposure no longer converts caller-authored compliance displacement into
  admissible force or release evidence.
- Wound debridement no longer accepts caller-authored force/speed dictionaries.
- Rescue no longer falls back to authored pressure or inferred pump/ventilation
  success. Rescue policy reward is zero until evidence is action-bound and
  complete across every active target.

### Misleading gates removed or narrowed

Smoke scripts and tests that merely fed authored values into the expected
answer were deleted or narrowed. This is not a reduction in valid evidence:
those paths could only prove that a formula returned its own prescribed result.
Manifests or qualification records affected by current source changes are
stale until deliberately regenerated against an executed revision.

### Generator scope corrected where source review proved destructive behavior

The exposure generator now audits both rendered runtime/evidence modules before
mutation, writes README/LICENSE/NOTICE only inside the asset package, and builds
overlay/archive content from an explicit exposure-only allowlist. It no longer
targets repository-root documents or manifests/zips the full checkout. Its
final directory synchronization is still delete/copy rather than an atomic
promotion, and no generated output was produced in this review. The independent
seal/divide manifest-overwrite defect remains open and is recorded in that
asset's section.

## Decisive unresolved source findings

These are promotion blockers, not requests for more tests or gates.

1. **Patient intervals are not atomic.** `DynamicSurgicalPatient.step()` now
   preflights the complete contact interval before advancing its clock, but
   authority time, contact effects, physiology, callbacks, and evidence cursors
   still commit in sequence without rollback. `PatientEventBus.emit()` invokes
   callbacks inside the mutation path. B requires one staged candidate for all
   patient/effect/resource state, one revision commit, then exception-isolated
   event publication.
2. **Patient outcome state remains directly mutable.** `PatientAuthority`
   still publicly exposes withdrawal/infusion/transfusion/output methods;
   `DynamicSurgicalPatient` publishes mutable tissue, bleeding, perfusion,
   respiration, cardiovascular, fluid, and damage models; rescue returns the
   mutable authority. `TissueStateRegistry.get()` now rejects undeclared
   targets, but returned records remain mutable. B requires immutable public
   views and patient-owned, interval-scoped mutation capabilities.
3. **Reported fluids are not fully conserved.** `record_bile_output()` and
   `collect_suction()` increment counters without debiting biliary, field,
   irrigation, blood, or urine compartments. B requires source-resolved
   transfers among intravascular, interstitial, biliary, urinary, operative
   field, irrigation, line, and canister pools plus a signed residual.
4. **Pump accounting can discard or strand measured mass.**
   `ContactDrivenResuscitationEffects` treats the first nonzero frame as a
   baseline, takes a minimum across displacement/flow/reservoir measurements,
   can zero delivery outside a pressure window, and accumulates
   `line_storage_or_unreconciled_ml` without a later drain route. B requires
   signed equality, reverse flow, quiescent initialization, recoverable line
   storage, and topology continuity.
5. **Ventilation is not gas-conserving.** Its source/interface flows are
   positive-clamped and effect gates can erase measured transport. There is no
   signed circuit storage, leak/exhaust, oxygen/carbon-dioxide, dead-space, or
   alveolar exchange balance. Pressure injury is not a substitute for gas
   conservation.
6. **Runtime identity is incomplete.** Python object identity and prim paths do
   not provide a stable patient UUID, reset generation, or monotonic mutation
   revision. Every patient-bound envelope needs all three, and resets/topology
   transitions need explicit receipts consumed by every workcell and sensor.
7. **Evidence is registered but not provider-authentic.** Core provenance,
   source, measurement, and envelope classes are caller-constructible. Typed
   patient/rescue objects now revalidate prim/contact-pair/attachment
   registration, but no environment-issued capability proves that raw IDs came
   from native post-physics sampling. B requires a provider receipt containing
   the collector instance, registry revision, native raw handles, patient
   identity, interval commit ID, and topology lineage.
8. **Autonomous Rescue exposes only a fraction of its modeled targets.**
   `AutonomousRescueORRuntime` publicly advances vessel, pump, and ventilation
   evidence; `RescueVesselSceneEvidence.target_id` is fixed to
   `rescue_vessel`. Bowel-anastomosis, abdominal-wall, and occlusive-film
   branches in `deformable_rescue.py` cannot receive public exact evidence or
   participate in one same-step barrier.
9. **Rescue actions, effects, resources, and terminal status are not joined.**
   Evidence names no admitted `action_id`; action records do not progress from
   request to effect/complete; non-pump tools are not consistently debited.
   The current zero reward is correct. B requires a single receipt chain:
   admitted action → exact tool/target → evidence batch → mechanics/topology →
   patient effect → resource debit → terminal status.
10. **Vasopressor and device injury do not reach the patient.** Inventory can
    be consumed without dose, pharmacokinetics/pharmacodynamics, MAP response,
    or clearance; extravasation and pressure damage remain local diagnostics
    rather than patient tissue/interstitial effects.
11. **Soft-tissue integration can commit an invalid configuration.**
    `physics/soft_tissue.py:949-1016` constructs an immutable result before
    commit, but does not validate every integrated position/velocity as finite
    or recompute element Jacobians after integration. A finite constitutive
    solve can therefore step into an inverted or non-finite mesh. B requires a
    candidate state, post-integration finite/positive-Jacobian checks, bounded
    distortion policy, and commit only after all checks pass.
12. **Rod stepping is non-atomic and its dissipation ledger is incomplete.**
    `physics/rod.py:528-885` mutates prescribed anchors, plastic curvature,
    edge damage/failure, twist, node state, and clocks before the final result
    has been accepted. Its linear/angular damping reduces energy but is not
    added to `dissipated_energy_j`. B requires candidate-only evolution,
    complete energy accounting, result/state validation, then one commit.
13. **A damaged vessel defect grows without a continuing damaging load.**
    `physics/vessel.py:912-929` expands defect area every interval whenever
    accumulated wall damage exceeds 0.5, even after pressure/traction has fallen
    below the damage threshold. B requires a declared residual-tear law driven
    by current stress/strain-energy release, or no further growth in an
    unloaded state; damage history alone cannot supply fracture work.
14. **Contact identity is not canonical at the type boundary.**
    `physics/contact.py:86-114` offers `ContactKey.canonical()` but the public
    dataclass constructor still accepts reversed/noncanonical body-feature
    order. `PersistentContactGraph` keys those values directly at
    `physics/contact.py:455-552`, so one physical pair can split history,
    deduplication, friction work, and dwell. B requires construction-time
    canonicalization or a private constructor that makes bypass impossible.
15. **Cohesive evolution can leave irreversible partial state.**
    `physics/cohesive.py:472-591` mutates damage, failure, accumulated
    dissipation, age, clock, and prior separation before `CohesiveResponse`
    construction/validation completes. B requires staging a complete
    `CohesiveState`, validating its response and conservation invariants, then
    atomically swapping state and receipt.

## Exact promotion blueprint by catalog asset

### 1. Legacy DrAnmar needle

**Current mechanism:** high-quality rigid geometry, mass properties, collision,
swage geometry, frames, and layered USD composition.

**To reach B source:** replace rigid-only dynamics with a curved
elastic-plastic beam or shell formulation that preserves the manufactured
curvature and supports permanent set, yield, and breakage. Couple the tip to
tissue through a rate-dependent puncture/fracture-energy law with explicit
pre-puncture indentation, breakthrough, shaft friction, bevel direction, and
withdrawal hysteresis. Couple the swage to the promoted suture rod with
load-direction-dependent slip and fracture. Report reactions, dissipated
energy, damage location, and topology revision from exact native records.

**To reach B evidence:** record repeated bend, grasp-slip, puncture, withdrawal,
and swage-pull runs with exact source/profile/asset hashes, solver settings,
raw force-displacement traces, contact records, host/GPU/driver identity,
convergence sweeps, and failure cases.

**To reach A:** calibrate and hold out physical needles across lots, sizes,
orientations, speeds, tissue analogs, and wet conditions. Require bounded error
for stiffness, yield moment, permanent set, puncture peak, insertion work,
withdrawal force, swage failure, and fracture location with uncertainty.

### 2. Braided 4-0 Warp suture

**Current mechanism:** nonlinear strand stretch, bending, exhaustive capsule
self-contact, friction, grasp transfer, swage failure, localized damage, and
strand failure are causal.

**To reach solid B:** replace contact-dwell knot strength with geometry- and
load-path-derived knot locking and capstan/friction state. Add objective
director/twist mechanics, distributed repeated fracture, topology-aware free
ends, braid compaction/crush, rate and wetness dependence, abrasion, cyclic
damage, and stable contact under timestep/substep refinement. Bind every run to
source, kernel, profile, GPU, and raw trajectory identity.

**To reach A:** calibrate on held-out straight tensile, multiple knot
configurations, wet/dry friction, cyclic loading, instrument crush, abrasion,
swage pull, and failure-location datasets. Report confidence intervals and
lot-to-lot variability rather than a single tuned curve.

### 3. Suturable tissue

**Current mechanism:** intact deformation and contact through a homogenized
linear-tangent route; puncture and topology-changing claims are blocked.

**To reach B source:** implement layered anisotropic visco-hyperelastic tissue,
rate dependence, hysteresis, near-incompressibility, and stable large-strain
contact. Add persistent puncture tracts, explicit needle passage, thread-tissue
friction, bite-position-dependent attachment, pullout, edge tearing, cut
propagation, and post-release wound recoil. Ensure failed elements create
topology that the thread and fluid models actually observe.

**To reach B evidence:** native mesh/order/timestep convergence; raw
force-displacement and topology history for indentation, puncture, thread
passage, pullout, tear, and closure; exact material and mesh revision.

**To reach A:** held-out biaxial, indentation, relaxation, puncture, pullout,
tear, and closure data across layers, orientations, rates, hydration, tissue
sites, and specimen variability.

### 4. Legacy hemostasis vessel and clip

**Current mechanism:** intact FEM vessel deformation and rigid clip motion/
contact. Pressure, flow, leakage, retention, damage, and seal are correctly
listed as non-promotable.

**To reach B source:** either integrate the shared vessel/clip mechanics or a
higher-fidelity FSI route with pulsatile inlet/outlet boundary conditions,
compliant wall storage, defect flow, conserved blood volume, and rupture.
Implement plastic clip forming around the actual vessel, serration contact,
measured tangential load/slip, residual springback, extraction/pullout, wall
damage, and pressure-tight leakage. Clip presence must never equal retention.

**To reach A:** instrumented pulsatile bench correlation for forming force,
closed gap, residual lumen, migration, pullout, burst/leak pressure, wall
damage, and repeated cycling across vessel and clip variability.

### 5. Laparotomy sponge

**Current mechanism:** rigid and deformable compatibility with separate dry/wet
presets.

**To reach B source:** add a conserved fluid ledger and porous uptake/release
state driven by local contact, pressure, saturation, and capillarity. Wetness
must change mass, inertia, stiffness, damping, friction, thickness, appearance,
and radiopaque-marker response. Implement compression/wringing, drainage,
folding memory, tearing, fraying, and retrieval-loop failure without switching
between authored dry/wet assets.

**To reach B evidence:** repeated native absorption, compression, wringing,
drag, fold, retrieval, and tear traces with mass balance and convergence.

**To reach A:** held-out measurements from representative SKUs across fluid
types, volumes, soak times, compression rates, production lots, and repeated
handling.

### 6. Standalone needle v0.3

**Current mechanism:** rigid half-circle needle with explicit geometry and
mass/inertia.

**To reach B:** consolidate onto the promoted legacy-needle backend rather than
maintaining a second rigid authority. Preserve this USD as geometry and
interaction identity, while the shared needle mechanics owns deformation,
puncture, friction, damage, and swage state. Add exact native evidence.

**To reach A:** use the same held-out needle validation matrix as asset 1, with
an additional equivalence proof that both catalog representations resolve to
the same mechanical authority.

### 7. Needle-thread v0.3

**Current mechanism:** maximal-coordinate rigid segments and D6 joints with a
provisional swage.

**To reach B source:** replace the chain as outcome authority with the promoted
rod/suture backend; retain it only as an optional visual/debug representation.
Add capsule self-contact, knot geometry, grasp transfer, distributed damage,
free-end topology, exact curved-needle mechanics, swage slip/fracture, and
tissue puncture/pullout coupling.

**To reach A:** jointly validate needle, swage, strand, knot, tissue passage,
and pullout so that separately calibrated parts do not hide coupled error.

### 8. Skin stapler

**Current mechanism:** credible trigger-edge, pusher, magazine, partial-stroke,
empty-state, and single-deployment sequencing. The emitted staple is preformed
and rigid.

**To reach B source:** model staple metal plasticity and forming against the
anvil, leg-tissue contact, puncture, crown motion, leg curl, springback,
retained bilateral attachment, tissue compression, wound load transfer,
extraction, pullout, malformed staples, partial penetration, and collision.
Magazine count must correspond to physical staple objects, not only a counter.
Deployment must report exact contact, energy, attachment, post-release load,
and topology evidence; `deployed` must not imply `retained`.

**To reach A:** instrument trigger/pusher/forming forces, staple geometry,
penetration depth, closure gap, tissue compression, pullout, malformed cases,
and cyclic handling across devices, staples, tissues, angles, and speeds.

### 9. Skin adhesive

**Current mechanism:** articulated applicator plus kinematic fresh/cured bead
task state.

**To reach B source:** implement conserved reservoir mass, paddle/piston
pressure, nozzle flow, viscosity/shear thinning, tip-contact and motion-derived
deposition, wetting/contact angle, bead geometry, cure kinetics, temperature/
humidity dependence, tissue adhesion, cohesive failure, peel, and wound load
sharing. Contamination, nozzle blockage, insufficient dose, over-application,
motion interruption, and premature loading must be physical failure modes.

**To reach A:** held-out rheology, dispense-force/volume, bead width/height,
cure-time, lap-shear, peel, pull-off, flexural, wet-field, contamination, and
aging data across lots and environmental conditions.

### 10. Stapler test cell

**Current mechanism:** bounded fixture motion and actuator/trigger sequencing.
Existing evidence is useful for mechanism control, not closure.

**To reach B source/evidence:** place the full forming staple and damage-capable
tissue mechanics in the cell. Measure exact actuator force/displacement,
forming work, staple shape, penetration, retained attachments, post-release
closure load, leakage/gap, pullout, and magazine identity. Reject prescribed
tissue nodes and attachment count as closure proof.

**To reach A:** correlate the complete test cell against instrumented physical
cycles, including partial stroke, off-axis, empty, jam, malformed, excessive
force, weak tissue, and pullout cases.

### 11. Wound Preparation Robot

**Current mechanism:** contact-derived dissipative work can now damage exact
debris bonds; release requires exact tool/debris contact, attachment identity,
raw records, and monotonic evidence.

**To reach B evidence:** implement the missing native provider for all registered
sources. Bind contact manifolds, tool/debris prims, attachments, raw tensor/
manifold identities, topology, and interval. Native release must remove the
exact attachment and expose the same topology revision.

**To complete B mechanics:** couple irrigation particles to a conserved
reservoir and tissue wetness; couple suction force and captured volume to exact
particles/fluid; model debris adhesion fracture, tissue abrasion, bleeding,
thermal/mechanical injury, clogging, overflow, and inadequate debridement.
Scripted suction and particle deletion cannot establish cleaning efficacy.

**To reach A:** instrumented irrigation flow/pressure, suction pressure/flow,
capture efficiency, brush/curette force, debris release work, healthy-tissue
loss, bleeding, residual contamination, and wetness across representative
wounds.

### 12. Atraumatic Exposure Robot

**Current mechanism:** all 12 capture cells must now provide exact contact pair,
contact area, normal/tangential force, slip, attachment reaction, attachment
identity, raw record identity, physics step/time, and registered ROI counts.
Transmitted load is the deliberately conservative sum of contact-resultant and
attachment-reaction magnitudes rather than a maximum that could undercount
co-directed load. Workcell, cell, tissue mesh, attachment, visibility sensor,
ROI, and calibration registration are locked for the capture epoch; replay,
fractional cell identities, reversed contact-vector orientation, stale
intervals, and oversized control intervals fail closed.
Exact post-physics carriage, lift, pitch, and compliance positions are required
at capture confirmation, and carriage/lift positions re-synchronize every
control interval. Caller-authored loads are rejected. Legal phase transitions are
explicit. Capture now uses a four-stage preflight → attachment authoring →
adjacent first confirmation → consecutive confirmation handshake. Preflight
identity/pose cannot age across an arbitrary step; per-cell force/area floors,
at least two confirmation intervals (three by default), and dwell are required.
All 12 contacts/attachments, the measured capture pose, and an authorized
topology transition must be present before retraction. Contact loss, excessive
slip, local overload, whole-pad overload, and missing attachments cause
fail-closed release and latch safe relief until an explicit, fully successful
recapture. Partial authoring is removed with a verified postcondition rather
than becoming hidden state. Fenestrated and microcup pads share the same
collision physics and are therefore labelled visual-only variants, not
comparative retention mechanisms.

These controls do not promote the physical mechanism above C+. During hold,
feedback uses summed normal contact force while attachment reaction contributes
only to the release-side transmitted-load check, so the controller can still
request more exposure while reaction load is already material. Carriage and
lift are synchronized continuously, but pitch and compliance following error is
proved only during capture confirmation. The legacy attachment fallback can
author automatic attachment commands without proving the explicit
overlap-selected vertex set. Tissue remains an isotropic surface-deformable
proxy with a small local fixture cluster, binary vertex bonds, and no calibrated
pullout, tear, perfusion, pressure-dose, or trauma law. The example is still a
visual spawn-and-step scaffold rather than the complete capture/provider/

# Mechanics

Major solid organs and selected body layers include explicit tetrahedral proxies. Membranes and bowel-like structures use surface or segmented representations. Layer separation, adhesions, cuts, sutures, staples, seals, and retraction use versioned attachment and continuity contracts.

The stable runtime may use cooked PhysX deformables. Exact puncture, fracture, cutting, and biological healing remain backend-specific and are not claimed by the portable layer.

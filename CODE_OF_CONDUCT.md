# Community code of conduct

We want Dr.Anmar collaboration to be safe, rigorous, and welcoming. Participants must communicate
respectfully, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, respect privacy and consent, and avoid
harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or sexualized conduct.

Research involving surgical robotics deserves particular care. Do not exaggerate clinical readiness,
share patient data, bypass safety boundaries, or encourage unsafe physical experimentation. Correct
mistakes openly and treat domain experts, clinicians, engineers, learners, and patients with dignity.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens people,
the project, or the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the hosting
platform. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as practical.
